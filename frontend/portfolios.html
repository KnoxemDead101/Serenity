Portfolios

Organize private portfolios and link existing Accounts as metadata only. Linking an Account does not prove its balance is cash-only or reconcile existing investments. Portfolio and container changes do not convert investments, move money, or change balances, net worth, or dashboard totals. Any approved conversion is a separate, explicitly confirmed workflow below. A portfolio holds your investments. New investments stay uncounted until review. A portfolio name (including a child's name) does not establish ownership or sharing.

Investment reconciliation and conversion

Review the private comparison first. A signed preview token is evidence only: it is not approval and cannot execute a conversion. Approval stores the exact report and corrections but does not execute. Execution is a separate explicit owner action. Original entered values are not live quotes; no currency conversion or price feed is used.

1. Match each original Investment

Portfolio-first drafts already have a portfolio. Choose an Account for balance review, and verify the exact instrument and immutable specification. A ticker or name is not proof of identity. Leave uncertain items unresolved.

2. Review linked Account balances

For each Account, say whether its existing ledger balance is cash only, includes investment values, or is unknown. For cash-only and combined balances, enter the cash portion in cents and attest to the complete set of Investments mapped to that Account. Cash-only balances do not include their values. An incomplete declaration blocks that Account group. No balance is changed.
